Why Dubai?

Because the answer is boring, and the boring answer is the point: no personal income tax, a licence in under a week, and a flight to two thirds of the planet inside eight hours.

The case, in four numbers

Sheet 02 • Fiscal

0%

Personal income tax

On salary, dividends and capital gains. There is no filing season because there is nothing to file.

9%

Corporate tax

Charged on taxable profit above AED 375,000. Below that threshold the rate is zero.

100%

Foreign ownership

No local sponsor required in a free zone, and since 2021 not on the mainland either for most activities.

10 yr

Golden Visa

Renewable residency for investors, founders, specialists and top graduates — no employer attached.

Average daily high, °C. Scale runs 0–45°. July and August are marked because that is when the city moves indoors and stays there.

Eight months of terrace weather, four months of air conditioning.

November through April is the reason people stay: 24–32°C, sea at 22°C or warmer, and every restaurant open to the outside.

June through September is the honest trade. Humidity above 80% on the coast, a 40-minute walk becomes a 6-minute taxi, and everyone who can takes their leave in August.
