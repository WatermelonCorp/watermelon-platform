The site

2.1 hectares, ringed by low villas.

The building

A slab narrow enough that every home sees water.

Maram is 24 metres deep. That single constraint decides the rest: one corridor per floor, cross-ventilation through every residence, and a west elevation that holds the creek from the fourth floor upward.
